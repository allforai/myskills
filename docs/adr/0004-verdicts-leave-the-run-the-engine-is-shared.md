# Verdicts leave the run; the evidence engine is shared

meta-skill's `/run` and cross-exam overlap in function — both capture screenshots, drive the running app, and ask whether a feature really works — but sit in different positions. `/run` verifies inside the delivery loop, as the author, and feeds repair. cross-exam audits afterwards, as an independent party that records and never fixes; its `examiner_is_author` bias-guard exists precisely because an author's verdict on their own delivery is not evidence. CLAUDE.md already orders them: a delivery pipeline first, then `/cross-exam`, then `/product-review`.

From this decision:

- **Neither calls the other.** `/cross-exam` stays user-invoked only and interactive at intake; a `/run` node never starts it. cross-exam never runs a meta-skill node. Each keeps its own entry.
- **`/run` keeps only gates.** A verification capability stays in meta-skill when its output is machine-decidable: a build passes or not, a contract is present or not, a screenshot's layout measures inside a frozen rule or not, a behaviour mapping has evidence or not. compile-verify, test-verify, spec-compliance-verify, security-verify, pipeline-closure-verify, quality-checks, runtime-smoke-verify, product-verify and visual-verify are gates.
- **Verdicts leave `/run`.** A capability whose output is a judgement about completeness or worth — "is this feature hollow", "does the product deliver the concept, 78 out of 100" — is removed from meta-skill. hollowness-detector is deleted; concept-acceptance is reduced to its coverage gate (every behaviour mapping has evidence or is named as missing) and its score, threshold and verdict go. Those judgements are cross-exam's and product-review's, made by a party that did not write the code.
- **One evidence engine, shared.** Build identity (commit plus working-tree snapshot digest plus artifact digest), digest binding of frozen inputs, in-app readback of applied settings, and reason-returning validators are extracted from cross-exam into a shared package mirrored into both platforms of both skills, following `shared/visual-acceptance`. Gates in `/run` emit evidence in cross-exam's ledger-entry shape — build, evidence directory, `probed_at`, `served_by`, readback — so a later cross-exam can verify and admit an author's evidence instead of re-probing everything, and re-probe only what author evidence cannot prove.
- **ADR-0002 and ADR-0003 stand.** Two independent visual reviews per screenshot gate remain a `/run` gate rule; they judge a screenshot against a frozen rule, not the product against its concept.

What this reverses: hollowness-detector's own text claims to be "independent of the generator" while running inside the generator's pipeline; concept-acceptance's pass threshold has been driving `on_needs_iteration`. Both gave the delivery loop a verdict it could not honestly reach. After this decision `on_needs_iteration` fires on the coverage gate's gap count.
