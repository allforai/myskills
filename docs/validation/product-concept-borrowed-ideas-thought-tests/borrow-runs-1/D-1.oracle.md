Success metrics (product-concept.json `success_metrics[]`)

1. **North star — Median first response time (FRT) ≤ 60 s (baseline 240 s), p90 ≤ 120 s.**
   FRT := customer's message → first outbound *agent-sent* reply. An accepted AI draft counts; an unsent draft does not. Window: rolling 4 weeks post-launch, ≥800/day, per-agent and team-median both reported.
2. **Draft coverage** = inquiries receiving a draft ÷ total inquiries. Measurable now; threshold needs the pre-launch baseline (unknown → do not fabricate).
3. **Draft acceptance** = drafts sent with no edit or cosmetic-only edit ÷ drafts shown.
4. **Quality guardrails, must not regress vs a 1-week pre-launch baseline** (speed alone is gameable by sending a fast bad reply):
   - customer reopen rate (new customer message within 15 min of the first reply),
   - CSAT / negative-feedback rate,
   - escalation/handoff rate.
5. **Semantic-edit share** = drafts edited for meaning ÷ drafts edited — separates "AI is good" from "agents rewrite everything" (which hides real FRT under fix-up work).
